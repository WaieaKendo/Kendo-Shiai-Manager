# KSM v3.15.11 — TV Context Stabilization

This release consolidates TV rendering into shared fixed zones and makes team-based TV views follow their existing Court Context presentation models.

## Kachinuki
- Three fighters per team only.
- Current active pairing and point letters appear above the lineup.
- Complete three-fighter lineups appear below.
- Lineup rows show only active, eliminated, or waiting states.
- No wins, points summaries, or extra statistics are added.

## Standard team formats
Three- and five-person team displays retain their Court Context lineup and wins/points summaries, with centered equal-width columns.

## Upcoming Matches
The next two court matches are always rendered as separate outlined cards, including unresolved bracket placeholders.
